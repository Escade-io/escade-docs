# Your first transaction

This page takes you through making your first sale or trade on Escade.

> **Trading is paused while Escade moves from blockchain to card payments.** The steps below describe how buying, selling and trading work once card payments are live.

## 1. Create your Escade account

1. Click **Signup**.
2. Register with your email and a secure password, then verify your email.
3. Go to **Dashboard** and click **Change Details**. Add your details, enter your postcode and pick an InPost locker from the map. You cannot buy or accept a trade without a locker, because that is where your cards are delivered.

## 2. Set up payments

- **To buy,** add a debit or credit card at checkout. Your card details are handled by our payment processing supplier, never stored by Escade.
- **To get paid,** you need to verify your identity and add a bank account. You will be taken to our payment processing supplier's secure form to do this. It takes a few minutes and you only do it once.

## Buying a card

1. Go to **Shop**, find a card and click **Add to cart**.
2. Open your cart and click **Proceed to Checkout**, then pay. You pay the price and a delivery fee. The payment is held until the card has arrived and been checked.
3. Wait for the seller to post the card. Its tracking appears on your **Dashboard**.
4. Collect the card from your locker and check it against the listing.
5. If all is well, click **Confirm receipt**. The seller is paid. If you do nothing, the seller is paid automatically when the 3-day inspection window closes.

If the card is not what was described, click **Return card** or **Dispute** before the inspection window closes. See [If something goes wrong](disputes.md).

## Selling a card

1. Go to **Shop**, open a set and click **Submit Listing**.
2. Choose the card, its variant, condition and, if graded, the grading details. Escade suggests a price where it has one. You can set any price you like, up to the £50 cap.
3. Click **Submit** and pay the listing fee.
4. When someone buys, the sale appears on your **Dashboard**. If you cannot fulfil it, **Cancel order** refunds the buyer in full, as long as you have not posted the card.
5. Buy InPost postage to the buyer's locker on InPost's own website or app.
6. Post the card within 7 days, then click **Input tracking ID**, choose InPost and enter the tracking number.
7. Once the buyer confirms, or the inspection window closes, you are paid the price and the delivery fee.

## Trading a card for a card

1. Go to **Trade**. To create a trade, open a set, click **Submit Trade**, mark the cards you **Offer** and the cards you **Want**, then click **Submit** and pay the listing fee.
2. To accept someone else's trade, open it, click **Add to trade cart**, then **Accept Trades** in your trade cart, and pay the agreed price of the cards you will receive.
3. Check your **Dashboard** to see who posts first. If it is you, buy InPost postage to the other trader's locker, post your cards within 7 days and click **Input tracking ID**. If it is the other trader, wait until their parcel shows as on its way, then post yours.
4. When the other side's cards arrive and check out, click **Confirm receipt**.
5. Once both sides are confirmed, or the inspection windows close, each of you is paid for the cards you sent.

Once a trade is accepted, neither of you can cancel it alone. Use **Request cancel**; the other side then sees **Agree to cancel**. If you have already bought postage, tell the other trader before either of you cancels.

## Where to get help

If a step fails, the error message starting with **FESC**, **BESC** or **RESC** tells us where it went wrong. Send it to us in Discord along with the listing or trade number.
