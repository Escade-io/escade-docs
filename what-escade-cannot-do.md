# What Escade cannot do

A marketplace is only worth using if you know its limits. This page sets out Escade's.

## Escade cannot verify what is in the envelope

No marketplace can. This is covered in [If something goes wrong](disputes.md), but it belongs here too, because it is the most important limit on what the dispute process is worth. Escade sees dispatch and receipt. It does not see the contents, so please take photographs and keep as much evidence as possible in case a dispute arises.

## Escade does not hold your money in its own bank account

Payments are taken and held by our payment and transaction processing supplier, a regulated payment provider, until a sale completes or is refunded. Escade tells the supplier when to pay the seller or refund the buyer, under the rules on these pages. We never move one trader's money to another except as payment for a card they received.

## Escade does not buy, sell or trade cards

We hold no inventory and take no position in any transaction. Revenue is listing fees. This is our source of income.

## Escade does not profit from failed transactions

When a sale or trade fails, the money goes back to whoever paid it, or to the trader who sent a card and was let down. It never comes to us. A platform that profits from failed transactions has a reason to want transactions to fail.

## Escade cannot undo a completed payout

Once the inspection window has closed and a seller has been paid, the sale is complete. Problems must be raised before then.

## Escade does not use cryptoassets

Escade previously ran its trades on a blockchain. We have moved to ordinary card payments so that Escade is easier to use and sits within the payment rules everyone else follows.
